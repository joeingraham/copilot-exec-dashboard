Cost & Unit Economics

COGS breakdown, per-seat cost, and gross margin

Total COGS i
Purpose: Total cost of goods sold over time, stacked by category (inference, compute, egress). The denominator for margin.
📌 Inference is 85% of COGS. Total growing but rate of growth slowing — model efficiency gains are kicking in.
(Illustrative Data)
COGS per Seat / Month i
Purpose: Unit economics north star — cost per seat must trend down for margin expansion.
📌 COGS/seat dropped from $9.50 to $7.20 — approaching the $7.00 target. Model routing and caching driving improvement.
(Illustrative Data)
Gross Margin % i
Purpose: (Revenue − COGS) ÷ Revenue. The "is this a real business" metric. Goal band: 65-75%.
📌 Gross margin reached 68%, entering the goal band. Driven by both revenue growth and COGS optimization.
(Illustrative Data)
COGS by Model i
Purpose: Model mix and how routing decisions affect total cost. Shifting to cheaper models reduces COGS.
📌 GPT-4 share dropped from 70% to 38% while Claude grew to 23% — active model diversification is reducing cost concentration risk.
(Illustrative Data)
Cost Percentile Distribution i
Purpose: Power-law cost distribution. Shows that top percentile users consume disproportionate resources.
📌 P99 users cost $85/mo vs P50 at $3/mo — the top 1% drive ~20% of total COGS. Targeted rate limiting opportunity.
(Illustrative Data)
